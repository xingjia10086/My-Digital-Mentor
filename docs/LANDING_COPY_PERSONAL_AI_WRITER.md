### Body

- articles
- newsletters
- blog posts
- journals
- notes
- research drafts

But when it is time to write again, you still face the same problem:

- your old writing is buried
- your best ideas are fragmented
- your tone is hard to reproduce consistently
- generic AI tools write faster, but make you sound more average

## 4. Solution Section

### Title

A writing system built from your own archive

### Body

Personal AI Writer helps you:

1. import your archive
2. retrieve your past writing by topic
3. learn your recurring tone, structure, and ideas
4. generate new drafts grounded in what you already wrote

Instead of replacing your voice, it extends it.

## 5. How It Works

### Step 1

Import your archive

Articles, markdown files, notes, journals, newsletters.

### Step 2

Retrieve your past ideas

The system searches your archive for relevant fragments, themes, and arguments.

### Step 3

Generate a new draft

Use your own archive as the source of truth for writing that feels more like you.

### Step 4

Refine and publish

Turn archive fragments into newsletters, essays, scripts, social posts, book chapters, or podcast drafts.
