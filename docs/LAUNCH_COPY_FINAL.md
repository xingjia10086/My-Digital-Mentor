## Final English Launch Post

I built an open-source project that turns your past writing into a personal AI system.

Not a generic chatbot.
Not another wrapper.

It uses your own articles, notes, and journals as the source of truth, so it can:

- retrieve your old ideas
- answer with your past context
- write in a voice closer to yours
- map how your thinking evolved
- challenge your decisions using your own words

I call it My Digital Mentor.

If you are a creator, founder, researcher, or anyone with years of writing, this might be a much more useful direction than generic AI chat.

GitHub:
https://github.com/xingjia10086/My-Digital-Mentor

If this idea resonates, give it a star.
